Passport Memory — Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Terms of Use

By using Passport Memory, you agree to these terms. You must be 13 or older to use this app.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Passport Memory has a zero tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Users may not post content that is offensive, harmful, or inappropriate. Violations will result in immediate content removal and account termination.

Content Moderation

All user-generated content is subject to review. Users can report objectionable content using the report button on any post. We review all reports and act within 24 hours. Reported content is removed immediately upon verification.

Blocking

Users can block other users at any time using the options menu on any post. Blocked users are immediately removed from your feed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Information We Collect

Passport Memory collects information you provide when creating an account, including your email address and travel memories you choose to log. Photos and videos you upload are stored securely on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the app's features, including storing your travel memories, generating AI recommendations, and enabling social features like following and liking trips.

AI Recommendations

Our AI recommendation feature uses your trip history, tags, and budget information to suggest travel destinations. This data is processed securely and never sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Public trips you choose to share are visible to other users. Private trips are only visible to you.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure. We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings menu within the app. This will permanently delete all your data.
