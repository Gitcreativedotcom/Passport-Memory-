Passport Memory Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Passport Memory is an iPhone app for keeping a record of your trips, sharing stories and talking to other travellers. Passport Memory is operated by Alex Hylton ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. This policy explains what the app collects, who receives it, and the choices you have.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

1. What we collect

Account. Your email address and a password, or the details Apple shares when you use Sign in with Apple (an email address, which may be an Apple private relay address). Passwords are handled by our database provider's sign-in service and are never visible to us.

Profile. Your username, and whatever else you choose to add: name, profile photo, bio, home country and travel styles. We also store your follower and following counts, and your privacy settings (who can see your stories, who can message you).

Trips. What you enter: title, country, city, dates, notes, activities, tags, budget and spending figures, a cover photo, and photos and videos.

Stories. The photos and videos you post, any caption and overlays, who you chose to show them to, and who viewed or liked them.

Social activity. Who you follow, your likes and comments, and the people you block.

Messages. The messages and photos you send, who they're with, and when they were read.

Reports. When you report something, we store your report, the reason you chose, and what you reported (see section 6).

AI recommendations and planning. Recommendations generated for you, trips you plan, and a record of when you open the planner.

Flight searches (Pro). The cities, dates, number of travellers and optional budget you search for, and the results returned.

Purchases. Your plan (Free, Plus or Pro), its status, how many AI credits you've used this month and your bonus credit balance. Apple handles payment: we never see your card or bank details.

Technical data. When the app talks to our servers, our providers record technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request, in their normal service logs.

We do not collect your device's location, and the app never asks for location permission. Photos you add to a trip's gallery may contain the location where they were taken, and we store it with the photo (section 5).

3. AI features

Inspire Me and "similar trip" suggestions are written by Anthropic's Claude model. When you ask for one, our server sends Anthropic:

  • Inspire Me: the country, city, tags, cost and activities of your trips.
  • Similar trip: the title, country, city, budget, tags and activities of the trip you chose. That can be your own trip or a public trip by someone else.

Your name, email address and user id are not sent. Anthropic processes these details to generate the reply, under its own commercial terms. The recommendation that comes back is saved in your account, so you can see it later, until you delete it or your account. An unsaved recommendation stops being offered after 7 days.

AI suggestions can be wrong or out of date. Check anything important before you rely on it.

4. What other people can see

  • Profile: your username, name, profile photo, bio, home country, travel styles and follower and following counts are visible to other signed-in users. So are your privacy settings (who can see your stories and who can message you) and the date you joined.
  • Trips: a Public trip, with its photos, comments and likes, can be seen by anyone signed in, including in Explore. A Private trip is only shown to you: it isn't listed in Explore or on your profile for anyone else. However, trip photos and videos are kept in file storage where anyone who has a file's exact web link can open it, including for private trips. The app never shows those links to other users. Photos added with version 1.2.0 or later of the app have long random file names that can't be guessed; photos added with earlier versions may have names based on the time they were uploaded.
  • Stories: you choose Followers or Everyone for each story, and your account's Story Visibility setting can make it stricter. Everyone means anyone signed in to Passport Memory, except people you've blocked. You can see who viewed your story.
  • Comments and likes you leave on a public trip are visible to anyone who can see that trip.
  • Messages are only visible to the people in the conversation, and to us as described in section 6.

5. Photos, videos and location

The app never reads your device's location. Photos and videos can carry their own location in the file, so the app removes it where it can:

  • Removed: photos in stories and messages, your profile photo and trip cover photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes the location and other details stored in the photo; if that can't be done, the photo isn't uploaded. Videos from your photo library are cleaned of location and device details on your phone before upload; if that can't be done, the video isn't uploaded. Videos recorded with the in-app camera are saved without location.
  • May be kept: photos you add to a trip's photo gallery are uploaded as picked, so they may still include location information stored in the photo file, which anyone who can open the photo could read. If you don't want to share where a photo was taken, use it in a story or as the trip's cover instead, where the location is removed.

6. Stories, drafts, messages and reports

  • Stories disappear after 24 hours. Their files are deleted from our storage within about the next hour and a half. A story you delete goes the same way. If a story has been reported and the report is still open, we keep it, including its file, until the report is dealt with.
  • Drafts of stories are saved only on your phone. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted if you delete the app. A draft is removed after 30 days.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted in transit and stored on our database provider's servers. We can access them if needed, for example to investigate a report. A message stays until you unsend it or one of you deletes their account. An unsent message is removed within about a day, unless it has been reported and the report is still open.
  • Reports: a report records who made it, the reason and what was reported. For comments, the first 500 characters and the author are copied into the report, so we can review them even if the comment is deleted. We review reported content and may remove it or suspend accounts (see the Terms of Use).

7. How long we keep data, and deleting your account

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete individual trips, stories, comments, messages and recommendations at any time.

Delete your account in the app: tap the gear on the Memories tab, then Delete Account. This permanently removes:

  • your account, profile, trips, photos and videos, stories, comments, likes, follows and blocks;
  • every conversation you're part of, for both people, including the other person's messages and photos in it;
  • your AI recommendations, planned trips and flight searches;
  • reports about your content, reports you made, and reports on messages in your conversations.

What remains afterwards:

  • Backups: our database provider keeps daily backups for up to 7 days. Deleted data can stay in those backups until they expire.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
  • Provider logs: technical logs kept by our providers expire on their own schedules.
