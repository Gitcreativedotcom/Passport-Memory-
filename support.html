Frequently asked questions

How does Inspire Me work?

Inspire Me uses AI to suggest a trip based on the trips you've already added: your destinations, budgets and travel style. Each plan has a monthly limit on AI recommendations. See Settings → Subscription for yours. AI suggestions can be wrong, so check anything important before you rely on it.

Who can see my trips?

Each trip is either Public or Private, and you can change it at any time on the trip's screen. Public trips can appear in Explore for anyone signed in. Private trips are only visible to you in the app, not in Explore and not to your followers. Trip photos are stored at web links, and anyone who has a photo's exact link can open it; the Privacy Policy explains more.

What is Flight search (beta)?

A Pro bonus, still in beta. It looks up current flight prices for a route and dates, for information only: you can't book in the app, and prices can change. You can run up to 8 searches a day. A search that can't run doesn't use any credits.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

Plus and Pro are auto-renewing monthly subscriptions, billed by Apple. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

How do stories work, and who can see them?

A story is a photo or video that disappears after 24 hours. Every story goes to Followers or Everyone, and your account's Story Visibility setting in Settings → Privacy & Security can make that stricter. Drafts are saved on your phone only.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ••• on a post, comment or story, or touch and hold a message, and choose Report. To block someone, use the same ••• menu, or ••• at the top of a chat. We aim to review reports within 24 hours and act promptly on content that breaks these Terms.

How do I delete my account?

Tap the gear on the Memories tab, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and your memories, and can't be undone. Copies may remain in backups for up to 7 days. The Privacy Policy explains what's removed.
